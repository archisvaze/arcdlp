Video codec
Best available quality
If a video doesn't offer the chosen codec, the best available one is used instead. H.264 plays everywhere but tops out at 1080p on YouTube. AV1 and VP9 give smaller files at the same quality. VP9 downloads are saved as WebM, the rest as MP4.

How it works
Paste a video or playlist URL, pick your preferred quality, and download. Videos are saved as MP4, audio as MP3.
Where are my downloads?
By default, files are saved to your Downloads folder in a subfolder. You can change this in Settings. Use the "Open folder" button in the Queue tab to find your files quickly.
Open source. Everything runs locally on your machine. No data is sent anywhere.
